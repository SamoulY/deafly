# Chart teaching and readable learning records

The home screen uses one BTCUSD price chart. Only its rightmost point can open a teaching example; the accessible latest-point button and Enter/Space on the chart do the same. Buy / Sell / Wait remain teaching labels, never direct orders. The server freezes the latest 40 one-minute candles and current holdings, and rejects an explicitly supplied older timestamp. Examples expire after two minutes. Saving and retries use the existing idempotent private training ledger.

Records explain the measured change in preference on each example. Autonomous decisions compare the actual frozen learned head with a zero-example head, holding market features, neural output and pre-decision portfolio constant. They distinguish changed choices, unchanged choices, inactive signals and unavailable evidence. Related examples were members of that version of the model; the UI attributes their combined effect, never claims an individual example caused a trade. Legacy comparisons are replayed only from a matching frozen checkpoint and accepted only when the replay reproduces the stored action. Record lists remain bounded to 100 decisions and 100 examples.

New anonymous identities start with a paper BTC purchase using a $10,000 total budget, including the existing 0.1% fee and 0.05% simulated buy slippage. Quantity is rounded down to 8 decimals and the remainder stays as cash. A fresh, closed server BTCUSD quote is required. Identity, portfolio, initial-allocation receipt and policy commit in one D1 transaction. Reloading or registering the existing identity does not buy again. Existing portfolios are not migrated. The opening allocation is shown separately from autonomous decisions and is not a training example.

Home uses a stacked mobile layout and, from 1,000 pixels, a spacious desktop workspace: account value and the larger market chart on the left; the original fly, holdings and latest shared moment in a right sidebar. It grows to 1,440 pixels while keeping the secondary pages at readable widths. Account-value history stays inside holdings details, with no extra home status cards.

Desktop exposes line/candlestick styles, nine intervals from one minute through one calendar month, and a settings drawer for MA20, volume and saved teaching markers. Each interval fetches 40 completed provider candles, rather than regrouping just 40 minutes. Kraken supplies minute through weekly charts; Bitfinex supplies calendar-month BTCUSD candles. Mobile starts with a line and folds controls behind the chart menu. Preferences are saved separately for the two layouts.

Teaching always opens the latest one-minute context, even when browsing a longer chart interval. The timeframe is frozen while an example is pending. Display settings, language changes and layout changes never submit orders or training, and switching languages preserves the draft example and its retry key.

The Reinforcement practice page selects a random server-owned cutoff between seven days and a year ago, requests real Bitfinex BTCUSD candles ending before that cutoff, and sends only 40 completed historical candles to the browser. No future price or outcome is sent. Missing trading minutes are disclosed rather than synthesized. Volume, MA20, price movement and practice holdings help the user interpret the scene. These examples train the same private decision head used by future autonomous decisions. The learning method remains bounded supervised demonstration updates; the page does not claim a new reward-optimization algorithm or guaranteed trading performance. Actual holdings are never changed by a historical example. Records identify historical scenes separately.

The header offers English and Simplified Chinese on desktop and mobile. It initially follows the browser language and persists an explicit choice locally. `pages/i18n.js` localizes the UI boundary using the centralized English-keyed `pages/locales/zh-CN.js` catalog, retaining source text so existing workspaces can still update their messages. Names, input values, market questions, IDs and raw reports are preserved; implicit option values are frozen before their labels are translated. Diagnostic payloads and external market names remain in their original form. Main dates and amounts use the active locale. Language selection updates the current page without a reload or network request.

Appearance in More offers system/day/night, persists locally, follows live system changes, and applies to charts and specialist pages. The original fly scene is retained.

The header now links directly to account login and a profile with a display name and six preset avatars. Registration requires Cloudflare Turnstile, verified on the server for success, action and hostname; missing configuration or provider failure rejects registration. IP rate limits remain in place. Registered accounts preserve the guest fly, holdings and personal model. Profile names are escaped and excluded from UI translation.

The production Pages site proxies `/api/*` to its fixed Worker upstream, so account cookies are first-party even on mobile browsers. Other cookies are stripped before forwarding. The canonical API namespace used by guest tokens, identities and checkpoints is unchanged. Older cookie-only accounts may need to sign in once again on the new first-party endpoint; the account marker prevents silently creating a replacement guest.

Room / Shop tabs sit above the fly. The shop has 18 clothes, walls, stickers and desk items, with category and owned-item filters. Purchases debit the existing paper USD cash; sales credit the displayed fixed resale amount (half of purchase price). It never sells BTC to fund a purchase. Initial legacy clothes are imported only once, alongside a starter lamp. Selling an equipped item removes it. Inventory is excluded from the home trading-account total and this is stated in the shop.

Migration `0014_room_and_profile.sql` adds profiles, catalog, inventory and a transaction ledger. D1 triggers atomically enforce ownership, funds and server prices, change cash and inventory, and increment the portfolio revision so in-flight autonomous trades cannot overwrite a purchase. They also adjust active runs' reward baseline by the cash flow: buying a hat must not penalize the trading model. Idempotent receipts survive lost responses and reloads. Existing portfolios are not reset.

### User-device / website reconciliation

Account identity, profile/avatar, balances, holdings, item ownership and the teaching ledger remain website-authoritative. `/api/state` identifies whether authentication actually used the signed-in account or a guest token. A signed-in account discards any leftover guest bearer token without claiming that guest's local fly. After restoration, requests carry the expected owner; a changed login rejects old-tab requests before they can write fly or account data and reloads the tab under the new owner.

Fly identity/private keys and full-brain IndexedDB checkpoints remain on the user device. The owner and canonical API namespace are unchanged. They are never downloaded, replaced, or uploaded by reconciliation. Fly name and equipped-item preferences now persist locally under the API origin, validated account ID and checkpoint owner key. A device with no saved preference seeds it from the website; subsequently local preferences win when opening the app, account page or shop. Empty loadouts explicitly mean no items equipped. Actual ownership always comes from the website: a sold or unowned item is removed from the local preference, without purchasing it or changing cash. Whole-loadout updates use an atomic D1 batch. Per-owner Web Locks serialize reconciliation across tabs.

Name and outfit choices are saved locally before synchronization. Failed synchronization preserves them for the next account/room refresh. Account-page details explain the split and report pending changes; home gets no additional status. Account profile refreshes preserve edits in progress. Full brains and cryptographic fly identities are **not** transferred to another device; website teaching heads and history remain server-backed. This release needs no database migration.

The live-chart button and accessible label now say **开始指导果蝇买卖** / **Start guiding your fly’s trading**. Guidance still freezes only the latest market point and cannot place an order.

Room decorations render in the original fly scene and the fly-view display; a side mirror makes worn clothes visible in the eye view. These cosmetics do not alter the market data sent to the neural encoder. Idle grooming and wing stretches do not press trade keys and respect paused / reduced-motion preferences. Native selects and options have explicit theme colors; account totals explicitly use USD.
